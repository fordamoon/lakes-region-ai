How our pricing works.

The plain-language version. Your written service agreement has the final word, and we'll walk through it with you before you sign.

Quick Win menu, pick one: Missed-Call Text-Back, Estimate Follow-Up, Review Booster, Appointment Reminders or Website FAQ Assistant.

2. Payment

A Quick Win is half up front and half when it goes live. Card or bank transfer.

3. Where we work

On-site Quick Wins are within 25 miles of Laconia. Beyond 25 miles, an on-site Quick Win adds a $150 trip fee. The Operations Package covers up to 50 miles. Anything under $1,000 is done remotely, anywhere in New Hampshire.

4. The Readiness Check

If the written plan doesn't find at least $750 of time or money over 12 months, measured in your own numbers, we refund it. Otherwise it is credited toward your project.

5. Texting and carrier approval

Texting systems need approval from the phone carriers, which can take longer than 14 days. We start on day one and tell you the date.

6. Who owns what

You do. It runs in your own accounts, and your phone number, customer list and everything we write for you, including scripts, prompts and templates, are yours. You pay your own software bills directly. Thirty days of fixes are included after it goes live.

7. The Care plan

Optional. Three-month minimum, then month to month. When something breaks, we reply within one business day. If you want to pause it in a slow season, we'll quote the pause fee when you sign.

8. The founding rate

The first five Operations Package clients get $3,500 in exchange for a short written case study. It stops at five.

9. Launch partners

Three businesses get a free Readiness Check and the founding rate. It is a 90-day pilot with agreed starting numbers and a written case study. Nothing is published without your approval.

10. Cancelling

Email us at info@lakesregionai.com. No forms, and no calls to talk you out of it.

Questions about any of this? Ask on the chat. Twenty minutes, free.
