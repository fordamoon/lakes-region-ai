About Lakes Region AI

Led by Matt Alighieri. Based in Laconia.

Lakes Region AI is a Laconia technology consultancy. We set up simple AI systems for the businesses your town runs on, at a fixed price with a finish date.

Matt has spent sixteen years building and running digital systems at companies like Apple, Pandora, iHeartMedia and Townsquare Media, and today he teaches non-technical teams to use AI for a living.

The tools finally got good enough that a nine-person shop can use them too. Not many people around here offer them at a fixed price with a finish date, so that's the whole business: we find the two or three jobs eating your week, set up systems that handle them, and show your people how to use them.
